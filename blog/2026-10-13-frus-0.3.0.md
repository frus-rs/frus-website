The first release since the crates went on crates.io, and a large one: three weeks of
reviewing widgets and painting one by one, and of adding the pieces that were simply missing.
It breaks things, as a pre-alpha release may.

<!-- truncate -->

## Basic widgets that were missing

`Center` and `Aligned`, the single-purpose boxes, `GestureDetector`, `MouseRegion`, `Listener`,
`OrientationBuilder`, `LimitedBox`, `AnimatedSize`, `AnimatedCrossFade`, `SizeTransition`,
`CustomMultiChildLayout`, `StickyHeader`, `SelectionArea` and selectable text.

## Widgets reviewed, one by one

The buttons, `Container`, the checkbox, the radio, the switch (its thumb can be dragged now), the
stack, the progress indicators, the app bar, `CircleAvatar` and `Slider` were each checked for
what they do, not only for their names. Visual density came with them.

## Painting

`BoxDecoration` in full: shapes, sides, lists of shadows and blur styles; linear, radial and
sweep gradients; pictures (`DecorationImage`); and the background's blend mode, with 29 blend
modes.

## Platforms

Each widget knows which platform's conventions it follows. This release adds scroll behaviours,
page transitions, the iOS back swipe, an Apple theme, and a desktop menu bar (on Windows, on the
title bar's line). Scroll physics gained never-scrollable and always-scrollable modes.

## Tooling

Frame statistics, logs in every platform's console, and an application template that builds
and ships to every platform.

## What breaks

The entries marked **Changed** in
[the changelog](https://github.com/frus-rs/frus/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md) say what breaks,
and how to move.
